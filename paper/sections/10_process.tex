\section{Pre-registration, deviations and reproducibility}
\label{sec:process}

The pre-registration, with the hypotheses, the grid, the fitting protocol and the decision rules, was frozen on
2026-09-23 before any training run, and its SHA-256 was committed to the repository the same day. The synthetic design
and its predictions were frozen on 2026-10-09, and the two supplementary plans on 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-15, each before
its runs. Every later change is dated in an append-only deviation log, together with its reason and its effect on the
conclusions; Appendix~\ref{app:deviations} summarises them. Before any data, the pre-registered training data
(sample-100BT) were replaced by sample-10BT and the pre-registered data point, the mean of a cooldown's last three
evaluations, by its final evaluation (Appendix~\ref{app:prereg}). On the synthetic task, before any comparison between
conditions, a curriculum was added because a uniform mixture of chain lengths was not learned, and modular arithmetic
gave way to lookup tables because modular chains were learned too slowly at this size. Two reviews of the results (2026-10-14 and 2026-10-15) found five
errors and then about 35 errors and overstatements, among them a verdict judged with a quantity the frozen design did
not specify; all were corrected and logged before this paper was written. A third review, of the paper itself
(2026-10-27), found two wrong counts, two rounding errors and a number of inaccurate, overstated or incompletely
disclosed statements, among them a misread synthetic prediction (P3a) and two readings fixed after the results that the
paper had not marked (P3c, P4); they are corrected and dated in the log. One evaluation that the
frozen design specified, the stopping rule of the loops = steps model, was computed but not reported until the second
review.

Code, configurations, the per-run results and training logs of all 232 language-model runs and 135 synthetic-task runs,
the fits, and the scripts that make the fits and the figures, are available at \coderepo; the remaining tables and numbers
are computed from these results. Fits and figures run on a laptop
CPU; the model checkpoints (485 GB) are not included.

\paragraph{Use of AI assistance.}
The study was carried out with the help of an AI assistant (Claude, by Anthropic), which drafted code, analyses,
documents and this text under the direction of \theauthors. Every decision recorded in the pre-registration and the
deviation log, and responsibility for the content, lie with \theauthors. Every number in the paper can be recomputed from
the results in the repository.
